High Performance Centre · Amsterdamse Bos

Cricket for all

At our High Performance Centre in the ‘Amsterdamse Bos’ we host cricket formats for all. From International matches, competitive, youth to social and fixtures. All year round training facilities in the #1 cricket club in the Netherlands.

01

ODI · T20I · Women’s

Internationals

VRA is the favourite location for the major international matches of the Dutch national teams, like the West-Indies and England in 2022.

02

Seniors · Women’s · Youth

Club competitions

VRA is represented at all levels of Dutch cricket. Our first XI competes in the highest domestic competition. We field 8 mens and 1 women's team and various youth teams in all age categories.

03

Zami · Midweek T20

Social cricket

In the social cricket league we host 2 teams on the Saturday afternoons and a T20 competition on Wednesdays.

Corporate · Expat community

Business

The club is connected to the international business community in Amsterdam and its expat community. We host several business cricket events each year.

Touring sides · World welcome

Fixtures

VRA has a long history on hosting fixtures. We’ve welcomed teams from all over the world. Come play at the best site in the Netherlands.
