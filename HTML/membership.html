Club life

What’s included

VRA is carried by its members. When you join, we expect you to help make the team and the club a success.

Training

Access to team training sessions tailored to age and level — outdoor in summer, indoor in winter (included since 2024).

Matches

Participation in competition fixtures during the Dutch season, typically late April to early September.

Facilities

Use of club grounds, nets, and pavilion — including the indoor hall through winter.

Coaching

Professional coaching and structured development across seniors, women, and youth pathways.

Umpiring & scoring

Members help the club run fixtures — umpiring and scoring are part of sharing the load.

Parents · youth

Parents of youth members support match days, lunch hospitality, and the pathway around the team.

Trial & grading

Try a session before you join. Grading for winter/summer groups runs in January–February.

Volunteering

Match days, bar shifts, and events keep Wagener running — every member plays a part.

Payments

Fees are managed via ClubCollect — one-time or installments. Cancel before 31 December for the next year.

Questions

FAQs on membership

Can I try cricket before becoming a member?

Yes. Contact us and we’ll arrange a try-out training so you can experience the club first.

Is there an introductory membership?

Membership follows the association year (1 Jan–31 Dec) and is for the full year. There is no separate introductory rate — trial sessions are the way to start.

Do I get a discount for a late start?

No. Membership is for the full calendar year and non-refundable.

Can non-playing members participate in matches?

No. Non-playing memberships are for support and involvement without match participation.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes. Via ClubCollect you can choose one-time payment or installments.

When must I cancel?

Cancellation must be submitted before 31 December for the following association year.

Is financial support available?

Yes. Support for families may be available through the Youth Fund Sports and Culture via an intermediary. Ask us for guidance.

What kit do I need?

Senior and junior competitions use coloured kit; Zami plays in white. Match balls are provided by the club and included in the membership fee. See the VRA shop for the 2026 kit.
